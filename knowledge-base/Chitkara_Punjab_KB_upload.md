## 4. ADMISSIONS PROCESS & POLICIES `[OFFICIAL]`

### 4.1 How to apply

- **Online:** https://admissions.chitkara.edu.in/login (register → fill form → upload documents → pay application fee → submit).
- **Download form:** from https://www.chitkara.edu.in/admissions/ after mobile-OTP verification. No Indian mobile? Email admissions@chitkara.edu.in or call +91 95011 05714.
- **By post:** request the form by sending a demand draft; return it by post/courier in the enclosed self-addressed envelope. No campus visit is needed to get or submit the form.
- **Book-store outlets:** https://www.chitkara.edu.in/admissions/availability-forms/
- **In person:** Chitkara Information Centre, Elante Mall Office Complex, Chandigarh.
- **Demand draft:** in favour of **"Chitkara University"**, payable at **Chandigarh**, any bank.
- **Last date** of receipt of forms is notified on www.chitkara.edu.in — check regularly.
- `[UNVERIFIED]` Application fee ≈ ₹1,000–1,100.

### 4.2 Admission flow

1. Check eligibility (Section 5).
2. Apply (4.1).
3. Selection by entrance-test merit or qualifying-exam merit, per program.
4. Written test / Personal Interview / GD, where the program requires it.
5. **Counselling — in person** (see 4.4). Bring the application-form photocopy and all originals.
6. **Pay the full fee at counselling** to confirm the seat.
7. **Register on the first day of the academic session.**

### 4.3 Admission policies

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Basis | Strictly on merit — entrance-test merit or qualifying-exam merit, depending on the program. Minimum eligibility must always be met. |
| Programs without an entrance test | Seats allotted on qualifying-exam marks plus any university test/interview. |
| Apply before results? | Yes, for marks-based programs. Otherwise proof of passing must be shown at counselling. |
| Rounding | Marks are **not rounded off** — e.g., 59.99% is **not** treated as 60.00%. |
| Tie-break | Equal qualifying marks → marks of the next lower exam considered; still tied → **older candidate** preferred. |
| Provisional admission | All admissions are provisional, subject to verification of eligibility and original documents. |
| Quotas | **No** management, donation, reserved-payment or payment seats. No capitation fee. No agents/middlemen. |
| One form or many? | Programs are grouped: one form per group; separate forms for different groups. |
| Incomplete forms | Not accepted. |
| Dispute jurisdiction | Courts at Chandigarh City only. |
| Dress code | Prescribed **uniform on all working days**. |
| Hostel fee | Separate from tuition. |
| Original documents | Returned after verification. |

### 4.4 Counselling & registration

- The candidate **must personally appear** before the Counselling Committee on the date/time given. **Failure = application rejected.** Nominees are not allowed; parents need not attend.
- Candidates who don't bring the application-form photocopy and all original certificates are **debarred from counselling**.
- **Registration:** on the first day of the session. A student who fails to register and stays absent **one week or more** is **struck off the rolls**.

### 4.5 Reservation

- **15% of seats** at Chitkara University, **Punjab** are reserved for **Punjab-domicile** candidates.
- SC / ST / OBC / Physically Handicapped / other reserved categories — as per State Government policy.

### 4.6 Documents for admission / counselling

Bring **originals + attested copies** (Engineering: **two self-attested copies**):

1. Class X and Class XII certificates & mark sheets
2. Graduation certificate & mark sheets (**PG programs only**)
3. Migration certificate from the board/university last attended ^
4. Rank card of the qualifying/entrance exam (Engineering: **JEE Main 2026 rank card**); if result not declared, the admit card ^
5. **Copy of Aadhaar card**
6. Character certificate — university format (Engineering: *from the institution last attended*) ^
7. Medical fitness certificate — university format, from authorised medical centres ^
8. Reserved-category certificate, if applicable
9. **Gap affidavit** — attested by Executive Magistrate / Notary Public, declaring no involvement in criminal activity during the gap (Engineering brochure: required for a gap of **one year or more**)
10. **Two extra passport-size photos** (not attested) with name & date of birth written on the back
11. **Photocopy of the filled application form**
12. Engineering only: **Bonafide certificate / sports or defence proof** from the authority, where claimed

^ **Items marked ^ may be submitted at the commencement of the session** if not available at admission.
Formats: https://www.chitkara.edu.in/admissions/downloadforms/ · Online uploads: PDF or JPEG.

### 4.7 Engineering-specific rules (B.E.)

- **Age:** only candidates **born on or after 1 October 2004** may apply.
- **Counselling schedule:** published on www.chitkara.edu.in after JEE Main 2026 (Jan/April) results. **No separate intimation** — applicants must check the website.
- **Seat allotment:** by the preferences given at counselling.
- **Laptop:** every B.E. student must have a laptop of the configuration published on the website (buy from anywhere).
- **Lateral entry:** diploma holders are eligible.
- **Admission window:** generally January to June–July, depending on JEE results and seats.
- **Cutoff:** none fixed; varies each year.

### 4.8 Mid-course withdrawal (NOC / Migration / Transfer)

No mid-course admissions are made to fill vacancies. So an **NOC, Migration or Transfer Certificate for mid-course withdrawal** is issued to any student (including child/ward of NRI/international students) **only after they deposit the balance years' fees** and all other dues.

### 6.0 How to read the fee tables

- **Program Fee** = tuition for that semester.
- **ETS\*** = Educational Technology Services fee, **₹25,000, paid at the time of admission** (shown under Semester I).
- **CAS\*\*** = Career Advancement Services fee, **₹25,000, paid with the 2nd-semester fee** (shown under Semester II).
- **TOTAL** = what is payable for that semester.
- **(Hons.)^** semesters = optional: students may extend a 3-year Bachelor's by two semesters after Semester VI to earn an **Honours** degree.
- `[COMPUTED]` totals add every semester's TOTAL. They **exclude** hostel, transport, exam fees paid to outside bodies, kits, uniforms and any revision.
- Hostel fees → Section 10.1. Transport fees → Section 13.

## 9. ACADEMIC RULES & REGULATIONS `[OFFICIAL]`

Source: Academic Regulations, Chitkara University, Punjab — approved by the Punjab Government (Memo No. 8/47/2010-3 Sec1/1356165/1, 20.11.2018), framed under Section 23 of the Chitkara University Act, 2010.
PDF: https://www.chitkara.edu.in/pdf/mandatory-disclosures/Statues-Ordinance-Pertaining-Academic-Examinations.pdf
Applies to all UG & PG programs; program-specific detail is in each program's **Academic Program Guide (APG)**. The academic calendar and live attendance are on **ChalkpadPro** `[TEAM]`.

### 9.1 Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Academic year | Two consecutive semesters (odd + even) |
| Semester | 15–18 weeks, minimum 90 teaching days |
| Credit | 1 credit = 1 hr lecture / 2 hrs lab / 3 hrs field work per week |
| Grade point | Marks out of 100 ÷ 10 (two decimals) |
| SGPA/GPA | One semester's performance |
| CGPA | Credit-weighted performance over all semesters, 10-point scale |
| Grade card | Issued every semester: course code, title, credits, grade, SGPA, CGPA |

### 9.2 Maximum duration

| Normal duration | Maximum allowed |
|---|---|
| 1 yr | 2 yrs |
| 2 yrs | 3 yrs |
| 3 yrs | 5 yrs |
| 4 yrs | 6 yrs |
| 5 yrs | 7 yrs |

### 9.3 Registration

- Students may register for the next semester **regardless of backlogs** (unless a program says otherwise).
- **Cannot register** if: dues are outstanding (university, hostel, any recognised body), previous grade sheet is withheld, or the student is debarred.
- Late registration: only with the Dean of School's permission + late fee. Unregistered students cannot attend classes.
- **Four or more consecutive weeks of absence** in a term → registration in all courses for that term is **automatically cancelled**.
- Registration can also be cancelled for unfair means, indiscipline, persistent non-payment, or registration not in line with the rules.
- To drop a term, get the Dean of School's **prior permission**; otherwise the name is struck off the rolls.

### 9.4 Attendance

| Level | Minimum per course | Possible concession |
|---|---|---|
| **UG** | **75%** of all held sessions (lectures, tutorials, labs) | up to **10%**, extreme cases only, **sole discretion of the Vice Chancellor** |
| **PG** | **70%** | up to **5%**, same conditions |

- **Duty leave** for approved co-curricular events at prestigious national/international institutions can make up attendance (cap set by the VC). It is a privilege, not a right.
- Attendance is taken in class and updated on **ChalkpadPro** `[TEAM]`.
- ⚠️ The 2026-27 brochure's general instructions say *75% attendance is mandatory for any university course examination* without a separate PG figure. The Academic Regulations (70% for PG) are the detailed rule — for PG questions, give 70% and suggest checking the program's APG.

### 9.5 Attendance shortage — consequences

1. **Debarred** from that course's end-term exam (a debarred student who sits the exam gets a null-and-void result).
2. Awarded an **"I" (Incomplete)** grade.
3. Must attend **extra classes** offered by the School in the **next semester on non-teaching days**.
4. Takes that course's exam in a **later semester** when it is next held.

Students can also be debarred for disciplinary action.

### 9.6 Evaluation

- Continuous assessment: sessional exams, quizzes, assignments, seminars, end-term exams.
- Theory: teacher assessment + quizzes + sessionals + end-term.

### 9.9 GPA / CGPA

- **GPA** = Σ(credits × grade point) ÷ Σ credits. Example: courses of 3, 3, 3, 2 credits with points 6, 8, 10, 10 → 92 ÷ 11 = **8.36**.
- **CGPA** = Σ(semester GPA × semester credits) ÷ total credits. Example: GPA 7.0 (12 credits) and 8.0 (11 credits) → 172 ÷ 23 = **7.48**.

### 9.10 Degree eligibility

- All prescribed courses cleared; thesis/seminar/dissertation credits cleared; minimum program credits earned (per APG).
- **Minimum CGPA:** **4.5** for UG · **5.0** for B.Arch · **5.5** for PG.
- No pending indiscipline/unfair-means case.
- **Outstanding dues → degree withheld** until cleared.
- In cases of gross violation found later, the Academic Council may recommend recalling a degree.

- *B.Sc Nursing?* → XII PCB 45% + written test + interview.
- *Is 59.99% counted as 60%?* → No, marks are not rounded off.
- *Is there a management/donation quota?* → No.
- *Is there a Punjab domicile quota?* → 15% of seats at the Punjab university.
- *Documents at counselling?* → Section 4.6.

**Fees**
- *B.E. CSE fee?* → ₹1,30,000/sem in Sem I–II, ₹1,50,000 in Sem III–IV, ₹1,65,000 in Sem V–VIII, plus ETS ₹25,000 (at admission) and CAS ₹25,000 (with Sem II). Total ≈ ₹12,70,000 for 4 years (tuition only).
- *What are ETS and CAS?* → ETS: Educational Technology Services, ₹25,000 at admission. CAS: Career Advancement Services, ₹25,000 with the 2nd-semester fee.
- *Can I pay in instalments?* → No; full semester fee at counselling, then semester-wise.
- *Payment modes?* → Net banking, debit/credit card, DD ("Chitkara University", Chandigarh). No cheques.
- *Refund if I withdraw?* → Before session starts: full refund minus ₹1,000. After classes start: pro-rata deduction + ₹1,000. Refund within 90 days of session start if applied before it.
- *MBA fee?* → Not in this KB; use the MBA fee PDF or the helpline.
- *Is hostel included in tuition?* → No (Section 10.1).

**Academics**
- *Minimum attendance?* → UG 75%, PG 70% (VC-discretion concessions of 10% / 5% in extreme cases).
- *Short attendance?* → Debarred from end-term, "I" grade, extra classes next semester, exam when next held.
- *Failed a subject?* → Reappear in a later semester; you're still promoted.
- *Pass mark?* → 40% (grade P).
- *Minimum CGPA for the degree?* → UG 4.5, B.Arch 5.0, PG 5.5.
- *Where do I see my attendance / academic calendar?* → ChalkpadPro.

**Hostel & campus life**
- *Hostel fees?* → Section 10.1 table.
- *Gate pass?* → Uhostel, 24 hrs in advance; return by 7:30 PM (girls) / 8:00 PM (boys); extensions via warden + parent confirmation.
- *Can I order outside food?* → Ucampus has the feature, but hostel students may not bring/order outside food into campus/hostel.
- *Uniform?* → Ucampus (not Uhostel).
- *Night attendance?* → 10–11 PM, biometric + register.
- *Laundry days?* → Two days a week, depending on your hostel.
- *Gym / library / medical centre timings?* → Gym 6–11 AM & 5–9 PM · Library 24×7 (Sunday till 11 PM) · Medical centre 24×7 with round-the-clock ambulance.
- *How many books in the library?* → 1,07,718+ books, 75,590+ e-journals, 711 research theses; IEEE, Springer, Elsevier access.
- *Is there a swimming pool?* → The official infrastructure page lists swimming among sports facilities.
- *What food outlets are on campus?* → Amul, Barista, Belgian Waffle, Blue Tokai, Chai Nagri, Dohful, Domino's, Keventers, First Coffee, La Pino'z, Subway, Venky's (plus the food court).
- *Fines?* → Vary by violation; general range ~₹500–₹5,000, can be more. Not a fixed chart.
- *Hostel refund?* → Babbage Block or Head Office.

**Transport**
- *Bus fee from Chandigarh?* → ₹43,000/year (2025–26 notice; may be revised).
- *Can I pay bus fee in instalments or get a refund?* → No instalments; non-refundable once paid.
- *Deadline?* → 15 June for the next session; ₹100/day late fee.
- *Which bus goes from my area?* → Look up the stop in Section 13.5 and give the route number(s), pickup time and "reaches campus 8:45 AM"; then tell the student their actual allotment is on UBus.
- *What time does the bus reach campus?* → 8:45 AM on every route (2026–27).
- *Is there a bus from Ludhiana/Doraha?* → The official campus page lists Ludhiana and Doraha in the network; the 2026–27 route PDF's Khanna region includes Ludhiana-side stops (Route 126: Cheema Chowk, Malerkotla Chowk, Samrala Chowk, Bikaner Sweets). Confirm with the transport office.
